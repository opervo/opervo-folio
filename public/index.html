Straight answers

Questions operators actually ask.

Can I bring my clients over from Jobber, Markate, or a spreadsheet? +
Yes. Import takes about 2 minutes from a CSV, and if you're switching from another app, send us a screenshot of your client list and we'll migrate it for you. Concierge migration is free.
Is there a contract? +
No. Month to month, cancel anytime in two taps. Annual plans just trade flexibility for a 17% discount; they cancel the same way.
What happens when my trial ends? +
You pick a plan or you walk away. We never asked for a card, so there is no surprise charge. Your data stays exportable either way.
Does it work on iPhone and Android? +
Yes. Opervo is on the iOS App Store, Android is in testing, and the web app runs on any phone, tablet, or laptop. Same account everywhere.
What do my customers see? +
Your brand, not ours. Branded quotes, invoices, on-my-way texts, a public folio page, and a private client portal with their job history and photos. No login required for them, no "powered by" upsell banners.
Do you take a cut of my payments? +
Never. You connect your own Square or Stripe account and pay only their standard processing rate. Zero Opervo markup, on every plan, forever. That's a promise written into how the product is built.
